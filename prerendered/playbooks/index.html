    The playbooks

    Six jobs worth doing this month.

    Start with the first one. It makes every other playbook work better, because the AI finally knows who it is working for.

    Start here / 30 min

    Stop re-explaining your business to AI

    A new chat knows very little about your business, so you get answers that could be about anyone's. Write one page about yours, save it where the AI can always see it, and the answers start sounding like they know you.

    1. Run one real task in a fresh chat and save the result.
    2. Write your one-page business brief.
    3. Add three things you wrote that sound like you.
    4. Put it all in a Project so every chat starts with it.
    5. Run the same task again and compare.

    Writing / 5 min a draft

    Make AI drafts sound like you wrote them

    Customers can often tell when an email came straight out of a chatbot. Six questions to answer before you hit send, so the time you saved does not cost you the personal touch.

    1. Could you explain it to a customer without the screen?
    2. Did you check every price, date and name?
    3. Is there one detail only you would know?
    4. Would you say it out loud that way?
    5. Did you cut what the reader does not need?
    6. Would you sign your name under it?

    Planning / 45 min

    Find the one task worth handing to AI

    Skip the AI strategy for now and work out which weekly chore to start with. This is the exercise behind our One Task Audit, written down so you can do it yourself.

    1. List everything you repeat every week.
    2. Note roughly how long each one takes.
    3. Score each for hours saved against what happens if it goes wrong.
    4. Pick one. Only one.
    5. Try it in the chat window before anyone builds anything.

    Sales / 1 hour setup

    Follow up on every quote without chasing

    Quotes go quiet because following up is nobody's favorite job. Set up a Monday routine where AI tells you who needs a nudge and drafts it in your voice. You read it, fix it, and send it yourself.

    1. Keep one list of open quotes and active customers.
    2. Give AI the list every Monday with one saved prompt.
    3. Get back who to contact and why.
    4. Have it draft each note using your business brief.
    5. Review and send. Nothing goes out on its own.
    Open the playbook
    Getting found / 1 hour

    Show up when customers ask AI instead of Google

    More people now ask ChatGPT who to hire the way they used to search. Find out what it says about your business today, then give it clearer answers to work with.

    1. Ask three AI tools about your business and your trade in your town.
    2. Write down the ten questions customers ask you most.
    3. Answer each one on your website in a few plain sentences.
    4. Make your name, hours and services match everywhere you are listed.
    5. Ask again next month and see what moved.

    Building / One afternoon

    Build your first small tool without code

    A quote calculator, an intake form, a checklist your team can open on a phone. You describe it in plain English and the AI builds it. We also tell you where the do-it-yourself route stops being a good idea.

    1. Check whether something you already pay for does the job.
    2. Write a one-page plan: what it must do and what it must never do.
    3. Build it by describing it, one piece at a time.
    4. Test it with five real examples from last week.
    5. Keep customer data and payments out of your first one.
