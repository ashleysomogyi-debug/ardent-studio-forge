    Workflows we automate

    What does AI automation actually look like for a business?

    Pick the part of your business that eats your week.

    Quote and proposal generation

    The problem
    Quotes take an evening to write, so deals stall while you find the time.
    What we build
    A tool that pulls past quotes, your pricing logic and the client's request into a draft proposal for you to review.
    What changes
    Proposals go out the same day in your voice, and you edit instead of starting from scratch.

    Lead intake to booked appointment

    The problem
    Inquiries arrive by web form, phone and text, and the ones answered late go to a competitor.
    What we build
    A system that answers each inquiry, asks the right questions, books the right calendar and updates your CRM.
    What changes
    Every lead gets a fast, consistent reply and you start the day with appointments already booked.

    A pipeline that chases itself

    The problem
    Deals go quiet because follow-up depends on your memory.
    What we build
    Follow-ups tailored to each deal on a schedule, plus a weekly list of the deals worth a personal call.
    What changes
    Nothing slips, and your calls go to the deals that matter.
